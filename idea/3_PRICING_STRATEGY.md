## Executive Summary

SocialToolkit will use a **freemium model** with three tiers targeting individual creators globally:

- **FREE:** $0/month (10 generations/day)
- **PREMIUM:** $9/month (unlimited)
- **PRO:** $29/month (unlimited + team + API)

Expected conversion: 3-5% of free users to paid within Year 1.

---

## Pricing Philosophy

### Core Principles

1. **Accessibility First**
   - Free tier must be genuinely useful (10 gens/day is real value)
   - No credit card required to try
   - Soft paywall, not hard paywall

2. **Value-Based Pricing**
   - Price based on value to creator, not cost to us
   - $9 = "coffee price" (guilt-free)
   - $29 = professional tool (agencies/serious creators)

3. **Creator-Centric**
   - Price for individual creators, not enterprises
   - Entry-level, not premium-level pricing
   - Grow into higher tiers over time

4. **Global Fairness**
   - USD-first, but consider regional pricing later
   - No location-based discrimination
   - Simple pricing (no confusing tiers)

## Tier Breakdown

### TIER 1: FREE ($0/month)

#### Features
- ✅ Hashtag Generator
- ✅ Caption Writer
- ✅ Bio Maker
- ✅ Video Ideas Generator
- ✅ 10 generations per day (daily limit resets at 00:00 UTC)
- ✅ Basic AI quality
- ✅ Mobile optimized
- ✅ No sign-up required initially

#### Limitations
- ❌ No save/favorites
- ❌ No export (copy only)
- ❌ No API access
- ❌ No team collaboration
- ❌ Max 10 generations/day
- ❌ Ads on page (subtle, not intrusive)
- ❌ No support (self-serve only)

#### Use Cases
- Students testing the platform
- Casual creators (1-2 posts/week)
- People who generate <10 captions/week
- Exploring before paying

#### Why These Limits?
- 10/day = ~70/week = enough for 1-2 posts/week
- Forces upgrade for serious creators
- Prevents abuse (bots, farming)
- Creates "aha moment" when they hit limit

#### Features (Everything in Free, PLUS:)
- ✅ **Unlimited generations** (no daily cap)

- ✅ **Export features:**

- ✅ **3 saved templates** (reusable prompts)
- ✅ **Priority email support** (24-hour response)

#### Limitations
- ❌ No team collaboration
- ❌ No API access
- ❌ No advanced analytics
- ❌ No white-label
- ❌ Storage expires after 3 months

#### Pricing Options
- **Monthly:** $9/month (billed monthly)
- **Annual:** $79/year (save $29, effective $6.58/month)

#### Monthly Billing Logic
```
$9/month = $108/year
Annual: $79/year = 26.8% discount
Annual: Best for serious users who plan to stay
```

#### Use Cases
- Content creators posting daily
- Serious Instagram/TikTok users
- Small influencers (10k-100k followers)
- E-commerce sellers
- Anyone generating 50+ captions/month

#### Why $9?
- **Market positioning:** Cheaper than all competitors ($15-49)
- **Psychological price:** "Coffee a month" mentality
- **Accessible:** College student can afford it
- **Stickiness:** Low enough to not cancel
- **Margins:** High profit at scale
- **Upgrade from free:** Natural progression

#### Expected Conversion
- Free users who love it: 5-10% upgrade
- Creators posting 5+ times/week: 20-30% upgrade
- New sign-ups: 2-3% upgrade immediately

## Revenue Model

### Monthly Revenue Calculation

```
Scenario 1: 100 Free Users
├─ Conversion rate: 5%
├─ Paying users: 5
├─ Premium at $9/mo: 5 × $9 = $45
├─ Minus: API costs (~$10), hosting ($5)
└─ Net: $30/month

Scenario 2: 1,000 Free Users
├─ Conversion rate: 3%
├─ Paying users: 30
├─ Breakdown: 25 Premium ($225) + 5 Pro ($145)
├─ Minus: API costs (~$50), hosting ($5), Stripe fees (~$11)
└─ Net: $304/month ($12.16 per paying user)

Scenario 3: 10,000 Free Users
├─ Conversion rate: 3%
├─ Paying users: 300
├─ Breakdown: 250 Premium ($2,250) + 50 Pro ($1,450)
├─ Minus: API costs (~$200), hosting ($20), Stripe fees (~$108)
└─ Net: $3,372/month ($11.24 per paying user)
```

### Year 1 Revenue Projection (Conservative)
```
Month 1:  50 free  → 2-3 paying  → $18-27
Month 3:  300 free → 10-15 paying → $90-135
Month 6:  500 free → 15-25 paying → $135-225
Month 12: 2000 free → 60-100 paying → $540-900

Year 1 Annual Revenue: ~$3,000-6,000
```

### Year 2-3 Projection
```
Year 2: 10,000 free users → 300 paying users → $36,000-40,000
Year 3: 50,000 free users → 1,500 paying users → $160,000-180,000
```

### Gross Margin Analysis
```
At 500 paying users:
Revenue: $4,500/month
├─ API costs: $100 (Gemini at scale)
├─ Supabase: $50 (database)
├─ Vercel: $20 (hosting)
├─ Stripe fees: $131 (2.9% + $0.30)
├─ Occasional support time: $50
└─ Total costs: $351

Gross margin: ($4,500 - $351) / $4,500 = 92%
```

This is **extremely profitable** at scale.

## Conversion Strategy (Paywall Implementation)

### Stage 1: Soft Paywall (At Limit)
```
When free user generates 10 times today:

[Notification shows]
"You've used your 10 daily generations.
Upgrade to Premium for unlimited."

[Two options]
[Upgrade Now] [Try again tomorrow]

← This is the main conversion point
```

```
Free users see (but can't use):
[Save to favorites] ← Premium feature
[Export as PDF] ← Premium feature
[Advanced AI] ← Pro feature

Psychological: Shows what they're missing
```

### Stage 3: No-Ads Messaging
```
Free tier has subtle ad:
"Ads help us keep SocialToolkit free"

✓ No ads | Ad-free experience
```

```
New sign-up offer (opt-in email):
"Get 50% off Premium for your first month"

Conversion rate: 8-12% of signups
```

```
For users who downgraded or left:

Helps recover churn.
```

## Price Optimization Strategy

### When to Raise Prices
1. **User feedback:** If users never complain about price
2. **Demand:** If conversion rate >10%
3. **Competition:** If all competitors raise prices
4. **Costs:** If our costs increase significantly

**Timeline:** Don't change prices for first 12 months

### When to Lower Prices
1. **Conversion too low:** <1% converting
2. **Churn high:** >10% monthly churn
3. **New competition:** Cheaper alternatives
4. **Market expansion:** Entering new region

**Strategy:** Rather than lower price, add more features to Premium

### A/B Testing (Year 2)
```
Test 1: $7 vs $9 vs $11
├─ Which converts best?
├─ Which has highest LTV?
└─ Run for 1 month with 10% of users

Test 2: $79/year vs $89/year
├─ Annual discount elasticity
└─ What discount maximizes ARR?

Test 3: Annual discount percentage
├─ $79 (27% off) vs $85 (21% off)
├─ Which maximizes lifetime value?
```

## Support & FAQ on Pricing

### Common Questions

**Q: Why $9 and not $5 or $15?**
A: Market research shows $9 is the sweet spot:
- Lower than Buffer ($15)
- Higher than cheap tools ($3-5) which feel unreliable
- "Coffee price" psychology
- High volume beats high price

**Q: Can I get a discount for annual?**
A: Yes! Save $29/year with annual billing ($79/year vs $108).

**Q: What if I can't afford $9/month?**
A: Free tier (10/day) is genuinely useful. Many creators never upgrade.

**Q: Can you offer educational discounts?**
A: Future feature. For now, free tier works for students.

**Q: Do you offer family plans?**
A: No, but Pro includes team workspace (3 members for $29).

**Q: What's your refund policy?**
A: 30-day money-back guarantee. No questions asked.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**
A: Yes. Cancel your subscription anytime in settings. No commitments.
